# Business: AI Game Studio

## Mission
Build delightful mobile games, primarily for young children, using a fully automated AI agent pipeline.
Liran provides a game idea → agents handle design, code, art, QA, and publishing.

## Primary Audience
- **Yuval (יובל)** and **Alma (עלמה)** — 5-year-old twin girls
- Games must be: simple, colorful, encouraging, zero reading required, big touch targets
- Win conditions: always positive, celebrate effort, confetti/stars/sounds on success
- No frustration mechanics: no lives, no game over screens, no timers that stress

## Secondary Audience
- General mobile game players
- Casual games, hyper-casual genre

## Game Design Principles for Age 5
1. One mechanic only — tap, drag, or swipe (not all three)
2. Instant feedback — every touch produces a visible/audio reaction
3. Large hit areas — minimum 80x80px touch targets
4. Bright, saturated colors — no muted tones
5. Friendly characters — round, soft shapes, big eyes
6. Short sessions — 2–5 minutes max
7. No text required — icons and sounds communicate everything
8. Always win eventually — adaptive difficulty or infinite patience

## Business Goals
- Build a catalog of 20+ games within 6 months
- Publish to iOS App Store
- Games hosted and playable on web via Games Portal
- Studio website showcases all games

## Revenue Model (future)
- Free games with optional parent IAP
- Ad-free experience as a selling point
